Ideal Electric Dipole in Uniform vs. Nonuniform Fields — No Damping

Field type
Center of massStationary

EnergyRotational kinetic energy
Ideal model: no rotational damping or friction. In a uniform field, mechanical energy is conserved and the dipole continues oscillating.
In a uniform field, the forces on +q and −q are equal and opposite, so the net force is zero. Their separated lines of action produce a torque that rotates the dipole toward alignment.
